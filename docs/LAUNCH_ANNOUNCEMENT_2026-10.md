# Launch announcement — iPhone app, the Netherlands, fresh German data

**When:** once the Netherlands is visible on schoolnossa.com (Lovable NL navigation published) and iOS 1.0.1 is live. Ideally also once 1.1.0 (Netherlands in the app) is approved; otherwise say "on the website" for NL.

**Facts used (verified 2026-10-24):** 2,824 German schools in 10 cities; admission info and open days refreshed for 2,526 schools (2026-10-03) plus official city registration rules; new school-year figures for Berlin, Frankfurt and NRW; every German description checked against the school's own sources (2026-09-28/10-03); crime shown vs a typical district; NL 1,629 secondary + 6,060 primary schools in 12 provinces. App Store: https://apps.apple.com/de/app/schoolnossa/id6808975096
