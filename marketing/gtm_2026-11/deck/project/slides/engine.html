Here is how it runs without you. Once a week, on Monday morning, a Claude task on your Mac does the thinking. It reads the stats and the new accounts, writes the next two weeks of posts from our data, checks every date and number against the database, renders the images and videos with a voice-over, sets up the next ads and uploads the YouTube video. The posts wait in a queue, two weeks ahead. Every day, a small cloud job publishes the posts that are due and pauses weak ads, even when your Mac is off. Meta's own spending limit sits underneath as the safety net. You get two lines in the morning email you already receive, and a one-page report each week.
