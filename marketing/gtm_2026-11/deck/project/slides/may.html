May: €120 Meta + €120 Google Demand Gen, 3–10 May 2026 · SchoolNossa GTM plan · 3

In May we spent two hundred and forty euros in one week, on Meta and Google, across fourteen cities. It felt expensive and converted badly, and the reasons are clear. May is off-season. The money was spread too thin. The message gave no reason to act now. Every click landed on the homepage, where visitors can't see any school data. And we only counted clicks. This plan changes each of these.
