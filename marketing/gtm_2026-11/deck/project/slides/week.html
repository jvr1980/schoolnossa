SchoolNossa GTM plan · 12

A normal week looks like this. On Monday, Claude plans, writes, checks and renders, sets up the ads, uploads to YouTube and writes your report. On Tuesday, the week's open days go out for the two cities with the nearest deadline. On Thursday, a deadline card or a city explainer. On Saturday, a feature demo or an explainer, and the YouTube Short goes live. Every day, the ad rules run, and the numbers appear in your morning email.
