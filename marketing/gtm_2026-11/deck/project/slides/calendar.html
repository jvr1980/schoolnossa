This is the calendar. Each light blue bar is an ad flight, and the orange mark is that city's registration window. Hamburg and Berlin start this month, because their open days are happening now. Then come Bremen, Düsseldorf and Köln, Dresden and Leipzig in February, Berlin, Frankfurt and Stuttgart in early March, the Netherlands in March, and Munich in May. The money always follows the next deadline.
