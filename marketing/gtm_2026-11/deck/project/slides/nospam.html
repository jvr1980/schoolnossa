SchoolNossa GTM plan · 10

No spam is enforced by rules, not by good intentions. At most three posts a week, one story a day, and one YouTube upload a week. An ad pauses as soon as people see it more than two and a half times a week. The spending cap is set in Meta itself. And some things are impossible by design. The access key has no messaging permission, so it cannot send DMs or write comments, and it cannot post in parent groups. We never upload user emails to Meta, we never email users, and we don't retarget website visitors.
