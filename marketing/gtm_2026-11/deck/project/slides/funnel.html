SchoolNossa GTM plan · 8

This is the path from a post to an account. Every ad, post and video links to a city page. That page shows the open days of the next weeks and the official deadline, without an account. Then comes the offer: the first five hundred testers get three months free. The link carries a short campaign code, and it is saved with the new account. So we know which ad brought which account, without a tracking pixel and without cookies. Every ad is judged by accounts, not by clicks.
