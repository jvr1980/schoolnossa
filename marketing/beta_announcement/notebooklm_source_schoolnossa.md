# SchoolNossa: finding the right school, with all the facts in one place

Status: experimental beta, October 2026. SchoolNossa is a non-commercial side project and still experimental. Feedback from testers shapes what comes next.

## The problem

Choosing a school is one of the biggest decisions parents make, and in most cities the information is scattered. The facts are spread across official statistics, the schools' own websites, city portals, police statistics and transit maps, and parents have to put them together themselves. Some of the most important things, such as when to register or when the next open day is, are often hidden deep inside a school's website.

## What SchoolNossa does

SchoolNossa brings this information together for primary and secondary schools and compares more than 220 details about each school. Parents can:

- **Search on a map** and see every school with their own places, such as home, work or the daycare.
- **See real commute times** from their own places to each school, for walking, cycling, public transport and driving.
- **Filter by what matters to them**: school type, public or private, languages, academic results such as the Abitur average, student-teacher ratio, transit access, safety around the school and much more.
- **Use the AI search**: describe the school you are looking for, such as "strong in music" or "STEM focus", and SchoolNossa finds schools whose profile matches.
- **Pin a shortlist and compare schools side by side**, with a chart that shows where each school is above or below the group average.
- **Read a short description of each school** that only contains facts that could be verified.
- **Discover similar schools** in the same city.

## What is new in this beta

### 1. iPhone app
SchoolNossa is now on the Apple App Store. It uses the same account as the website and has a map, search, filters, school details and the pinned shortlist.

### 2. SchoolNossa in Claude and ChatGPT
With the new connectors, parents can use SchoolNossa inside the AI assistants Claude and ChatGPT. They ask in plain language, for example "Find Gymnasien in Berlin-Pankow" or "Which secondary schools in Utrecht offer VWO?", and the assistant searches SchoolNossa's data. It can show school details, find schools that match a description, manage the pinned shortlist and give travel times from the user's saved places. Parents only see school data and their own shortlist; SchoolNossa never shares passwords with the assistant.

### 3. Admission information and open days
When available, SchoolNossa shows each school's application window, admission criteria and upcoming open days, the facts parents asked for most. SchoolNossa reads them from the schools' own websites and only keeps an item when it can find the exact wording on the school's site. Past dates are removed automatically. Right now, more than 1,100 German schools have an upcoming open day listed. For every covered German city, SchoolNossa also shows the official registration periods and rules of the city.

### 4. More German cities
SchoolNossa started in Berlin. It now covers 10 German cities with more than 2,800 primary and secondary schools: Berlin, Bremen, Cologne (Köln), Dresden, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Leipzig, Munich (München) and Stuttgart.

### 5. The Netherlands (alpha)
SchoolNossa's first step outside Germany: more than 7,600 primary and secondary schools across the whole of the Netherlands. Parents choose a province and up to 10 municipalities, and can filter secondary schools by track, such as VMBO, HAVO and VWO. The Dutch version is an alpha release, so some details that exist for German cities, such as admission information and open days, are not available yet.

## Where the data comes from

SchoolNossa uses open data: official school registers, education statistics, police crime statistics, transit data and OpenStreetMap. It adds what the schools publish on their own websites. Safety is shown as a rate per 1,000 residents compared with a typical district in the same city, not as raw numbers. Every value shows which school year it comes from.

## Important: experimental

SchoolNossa is a purely experimental project and not a commercial product. The data can be incomplete or out of date, so parents should always confirm important details, such as registration dates, directly with the school. Feedback is very welcome: what is missing, what is wrong, and what helps.
